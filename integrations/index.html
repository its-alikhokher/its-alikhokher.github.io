Shipping & logistics

FedEx shipping integration for ERPNext v16

A direct connection to FedEx's own REST APIs, built against FedEx's published OpenAPI models. There is no aggregator or reseller in the middle, so rates and labels are FedEx's own. It follows ERPNext's standard flow: Sales Order, Delivery Note, Shipment.

  • Rate shopping on the Sales Order and Delivery Note, with estimated cost, delivery date and transit time, including Saturday and Sunday delivery where FedEx offers it.
  • Shipping charge on the invoice. The chosen rate is added to Taxes and Charges and carries through to the Delivery Note and Sales Invoice.
  • Shipping rules for all customers, with overrides per customer or customer group, decide the default service and whether a FedEx charge is required.
  • Labels in PDF, PNG, ZPL or EPL. Multi-box shipments get one tracking number per box and print in a single file.
  • International customs: commodity lines with HS codes and country of origin from the Item master, and the commercial invoice generated by FedEx.
  • Address validation and tracking. A scheduled sync writes FedEx scan history onto the Shipment (Picked Up, In Transit, Out for Delivery, Delivered) and can email the customer.
  • Readable errors. Every failure shows a plain message; every FedEx request and reply goes to an API log.
  • Python
  • ERPNext v16
  • FedEx REST API
  • OAuth 2.0
  • Scheduler

E-commerce

Shopify multi-store integration for ERPNext

A standalone Frappe app with no store-specific code. It runs one Shopify store or twenty: every store is one record that posts into its own company, warehouse, cost center and accounts.

  • Order placed: a Sales Order in the store's company, lines matched to Items by SKU, with Shopify's own prices, taxes and shipping.
  • Order paid: a Sales Invoice and a Payment Entry into the store's bank or cash account.
  • Delivery Note submitted: the order is fulfilled on Shopify with tracking, and Shopify emails the customer.
  • Stock changes: only the levels that moved since the last run are pushed to each Shopify location, on each store's own interval.
  • Products and variants mapped to ERPNext Items, and Shopify customer tags mapped to customer groups.
  • Safe by design: every event is written to a sync log (Queued, Success, Skipped, Error), and a webhook that arrives twice never creates anything twice.
  • Shopify Admin API
  • Webhooks

  • ERPNext v16

Payments

Card payment gateways: EMS & MagicPay QuickClick

Card payments for ERPNext 16 through hosted QuickClick payment pages, used by the B2B portal and by staff taking payments over the phone.

  • Pay links on a Sales Invoice, Sales Order or Payment Request: a signed link the customer opens without logging in, for exactly the amount owed.
  • The transaction is read back from the gateway's Query API on return or by webhook, and the Payment Entry is created and submitted.
  • Refund and void from the transaction record, with matching accounting entries.
  • Hourly sync catches payments made outside ERPNext or missed by the webhook, and moves Approved to Settled.
  • Connection test and status report in settings, so credentials can be checked before going live.

Integration questions

Can ERPNext be integrated with NPHIES in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. I integrated a Frappe/ERPNext clinic management system with NPHIES for insurance eligibility checks, pre-authorization requests and claim submission, so reception and billing staff work from ERPNext instead of a separate portal.

Which payment gateways can you connect to ERPNext?

Moyasar for Saudi Arabia (mada and cards), and card gateways with hosted payment pages such as EMS and MagicPay QuickClick. Payments are confirmed with the gateway and booked as Payment Entries automatically, and card numbers never touch ERPNext.

Can ERPNext connect directly to FedEx?

Yes. The integration talks straight to FedEx's REST APIs with no aggregator: live rates, labels, customs documents, address validation and tracking inside the Sales Order, Delivery Note and Shipment.

Can one ERPNext site sync more than one Shopify store?

Yes. Each Shopify store is one record with its own company, warehouse, cost center and accounts, and orders, payments, fulfilments and inventory sync separately for each store.

Can you build an integration for a system that is not listed here?

Yes. If the system has a REST API or webhooks, it can be connected to ERPNext. My integrations follow the same pattern: a settings DocType for credentials and a connection test, webhook endpoints, a scheduled sync for anything missed, and a log of every call.
